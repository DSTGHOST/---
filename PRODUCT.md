# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Israeli women, roughly 28–55, arriving mostly from Facebook ads on a phone. They live in a rental, a new home, or a just-renovated one and feel the home "doesn't speak to them". They lack design know-how, not taste, and are wary of big spend, renovation mess, and losing workdays. The job: decide, in one scroll session, that a one-day makeover with Gal is worth a phone call.

## Product Purpose
Single landing page for Gal Zarhi's "יום מייקאובר" — a full home-styling day (consultation, measuring, plan, shopping list, shopping together) delivered in 8 hours. Success is a lead: name + phone submitted, or a WhatsApp chat started. The page follows a fixed 19-section persuasion structure (promise → objections → proof → what-if → dream → pain → no-blame → guide → process → differentiation → who-for → why-me → price → urgency → form → about → third proof → form → FAQ).

## Brand Personality
Luxurious but accessible, warm, personal. Speaks like a trusted friend in second-person feminine Hebrew, short lines. Emotional goal: "this is for someone like me", calm confidence, a touch of aspiration — never pressure.

## Anti-references
- Shouty course/sales landing pages: red everywhere, flashing timers, fake scarcity counters, exclamation-mark walls.
- Generic designer template sites (Wix/Elementor portfolio grids) with no story.

## Design Principles
1. Proof before ask — show real before/after before requesting trust.
2. One promise, repeated — "8 שעות" is the spine; every section reinforces it.
3. Honest urgency only — limits must be real (monthly capacity), numbers must add up to the shekel.
4. Read in one thumb-scroll — each section fits a phone screen; short lines.
5. Quiet luxury carries the warmth — dark + gold restraint, warmth comes from copy and imagery, not loudness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Must meet Israeli Standard 5568 (WCAG 2.x AA): accessibility widget and statement page exist. Body text contrast ≥4.5:1, RTL throughout, respect prefers-reduced-motion, touch targets ≥44px.
